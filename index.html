NextCuatro v5.13 Beta · Flexible. Smart. Adaptive.

NextCuatro organizes rotating pickleball games as players arrive, take breaks, and leave. Set the available courts, choose a rotation mode, add players, and press Next Cuatro! to create the next game.

What’s new in v5.13 Beta

Fixed an issue found in changing-attendance testing: if one member of a fixed pair leaves, their remaining partner can still be scheduled as a solo player. The pair preference is retained if the departed player later returns. Added evolving-session regression testing with multiple late arrivals, departures, pairs, skill changes and larger groups.

Corrected pair-fairness invitations when players join or leave mid-session: fixed pairs can be offered a temporary break when they have fewer adjusted sit-outs than the typical solo player. Re-invitations wait until the same solo players have each taken another break. Actual sit-out statistics remain unchanged.

Replaced the 50-player registration hard stop with a one-time advisory when the 51st active player joins or is restored. More than 50 active players are permitted, but performance and fairness above 50 have not been fully tested. The prior fixed-pair scheduling correction remains in place.

Previously fixed a changing-attendance edge case: if all courts are full, an approved temporary pair separation waits until a sit-out spot opens. Pair integrity, existing rotation capacity, and actual sit-out totals are preserved. Includes support for up to 8 courts and stress-tested scheduling with up to 50 active players.

  • Late arrivals get first-game priority and an arrival-adjusted scheduling baseline; actual sit-out totals remain unchanged.
  • Clear, custom pair-fairness dialog with Yes/No buttons; after a pair accepts one break each, no catch-up prompts until the same incumbent solo players have sat out another time.
  • Voluntary sit-outs count toward the same fairness total as assigned breaks.
  • Rotation and pairing handling strengthened for changing attendance.

Players & availability

  • Add players before or during a session; new players receive priority in the next rotation when space permits, without catching up on earlier sit-outs.
  • Choose a self-reported skill level, or leave a player Unrated.
  • Pair two players who want to play together, or unpair them.
  • Use Sit Out for a one-game break, or Leave when someone is done for the session.
  • See the number of active players at a glance. NextCuatro has been tested with up to 50 active players; additional players are allowed after a one-time warning, but larger sessions are not yet validated.

Rotation modes

  • Smart Random: rotates players while considering sit-out fairness and teammate variety.
  • Similar Skill: groups players with broadly similar skill levels.
  • Balanced Teams: aims to create competitive, evenly matched teams.
  • Adjust the number of available courts as attendance changes.

Fairness & fixed pairs

  • Rotation considers arrival-adjusted scheduling sit-outs, voluntary breaks, and consecutive games played; displayed sit-out totals are always actual counts. The app avoids repeat assigned breaks while eligible players have fewer sit-outs whenever pairing and court capacity permit.
  • Fixed pairs normally play together and, when possible, sit out together.
  • When a one-person break would unfairly favor a fixed pair, the organizer may be asked whether to separate the pair temporarily for two games. Each partner takes one actual break; after either declining or completing an approved two-game separation, the organizer is not asked again until every active solo player has taken another break. Actual sit-out totals are always preserved, even when a pair declines several times. Pairing and unpairing can be changed during a session.

Games, results & ratings

  • Press Next Cuatro! to generate the next game and court assignments.
  • Tap the winning team to record a result; the winner is highlighted.
  • Review games played, sit-outs, wins and losses, and partner variety in Session fairness.
  • Adaptive ratings can be toggled on or off for each player. Players who enter a baseline skill rating can have their rating adjusted upward or downward based on recorded wins and losses against other rated players. Players without a baseline rating cannot participate in adaptive rating adjustments.

Timer & session controls

  • Select a 10-, 12-, or 15-minute game timer; start, pause, or reset it.
  • At zero, hear the end-of-game chimes and see the end-of-game message.
  • Next Cuatro! resets and stops the timer for the new game; it does not start automatically.
  • Session data is stored in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data may remove it.
  • New Session / Clear Everything requires confirmation and erases the current session.
